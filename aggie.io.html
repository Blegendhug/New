Aggie.io - Collaborative Drawing

Everything you need to know about Aggie.io.

Aggie.io is an online collaborative drawing platform designed for artists and teams. Visit: https://aggie.io/

It allows multiple users to draw simultaneously on the same canvas in real-time.

The platform is ideal for brainstorming, concept art, and remote collaboration.

Aggie.io runs directly in the browser, no installation required.

Users can create private or public drawing sessions by sharing a unique link.

The interface offers a wide range of tools, including brushes, layers, and text.

Artists can zoom in and out, pan the canvas, and customize brush settings.

It supports pressure sensitivity for tablets, enhancing control and detail.

The layer system helps organize complex artworks efficiently.

Finished artwork can be exported as PNG or PSD files for further editing.

Aggie.io is free, making it accessible for both amateurs and professionals.

It’s used widely in online art communities and digital workshops.

Collaborators can communicate using the built-in chat feature.

Undo and redo options help recover from mistakes easily.

Users can customize canvas size and resolution to fit their needs.

The minimalist design ensures an intuitive and focused drawing experience.

It’s popular among game developers for quick prototyping of ideas.

Streamers and educators use Aggie.io to engage their audiences creatively.

Aggie.io fosters creativity, teamwork, and real-time artistic expression.

Overall, Aggie.io stands out as one of the best collaborative drawing platforms available today.
