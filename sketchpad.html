Sketchpad - Online Drawing

Everything you need to know about Sketchpad.

Sketchpad is a powerful online drawing application designed for both beginners and professional artists. Visit: https://sketch.io/sketchpad/

With a wide variety of tools including brushes, shapes, and text, Sketchpad allows you to create stunning digital art right from your browser.

Sketchpad supports layers, making it easy to manage complex artworks and edits.

The interface is user-friendly and intuitive, catering to artists of all skill levels.

One standout feature is the ability to export artwork in multiple formats, including PNG, JPG, and SVG.

Sketchpad’s color picker and gradients offer artists flexibility and creativity in coloring their work.

For designers, the shape library includes everything from basic forms to arrows and speech bubbles.

Sketchpad works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and even smartphones, making it incredibly versatile.

It also offers an autosave function to prevent losing your progress during a session.

Many educators use Sketchpad in classrooms to teach art and design principles to students.

Users can import images and layer them with drawings or annotations.

The tool’s symmetry and mirror effects open up advanced artistic possibilities.

Sketchpad is completely free to use, with optional paid plans offering more features.

Its offline mode allows artists to continue creating without an internet connection.

Because it's web-based, there’s no need to install or update software.

The undo/redo function makes experimenting safe and reversible.

Artists can save their work locally or share it directly to social media.

Sketchpad has a vibrant online community where users showcase their creations.

With continuous updates, the platform keeps improving based on user feedback.

Overall, Sketchpad is a must-try for anyone looking to explore their creativity online.
